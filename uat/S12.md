## Findings

**The verifier returned FAIL with five blocking findings and every one was
real.** The worst was not a security hole but a lie the product told about
itself: `gateStates` had no branch for a drafted answer, so an unconfirmed
proposal became indistinguishable from a person's own — it pre-selected the
button on the very screen reading "not your answer yet", counted towards
"every risk area has an answer", drove routing, and **vanished from the gaps
a person confirms at submission**. They would have signed the declaration
over a model's suggestion believing it was their record.

**Nothing validated the proposed value.** Anything not "Yes" reads as "No"
downstream, so a hedge — _"Probably, if the pilot expands"_ — closed an
entire risk area, and the card offered a button labelled with the sentence.

**Both new write paths bypassed the submission lock** and gated on
`canAnswer`, so a proposal could be accepted twenty hours after the
declaration, and an assessor could record a Tier-1 answer on somebody else's
assessment indistinguishably from the owner's own.

**A policy breach rendered as "Enhancement" on the requester's own submit
screen** — the third finding kind had no branch there, so this slice made
that screen _less_ accurate than before it, on the demo path.

**And the one that stings: G-65 described a guardrail that had never run.**
`contextualGuardrail` was imported into the drafting gate and never called;
an unused import passes the type checker. The skill repeated the claim. The
rule earned: **a guardrail is not shipped when it is written, it is shipped
when something proves it fires.**

Both guardrails were also nearly ornamental — `utteredInternalIdentifier`
claimed in its own docstring to catch `initial.surname` and could not, and
`claimsUnrecordedAnswer` compared whole sentences so **one true clause
laundered every false claim beside it**. Every string the verifier reported
as missed is now a test.

## Agentic opportunity

The §22.1 register still holds ten unbuilt features, and this slice makes
two of them cheap: **precedent suggestion** and **divergence signal** both
need the same thing — aggregate reads over attested answers — and the
attestation record now exists. The guardrail to carry forward is the one
this slice learned twice: a capability must be **absent or failing open**
when the model is not there, and which of those two it is should be decided
before it is built, not after.

Deliberately **not** actioned:

  where §22.1 says findings are raised against attested answers. Harmless

  no code change. Recorded rather than fixed, because the fix is a decision
  about when compliance checking runs, not a patch.
