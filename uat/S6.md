## Agentic opportunity

This is the strongest surface in the product for §22.1's drafting
capability. A control question — "are access rights periodically reviewed
and recertified?" — is exactly the kind a policy document already answers,
and the note a person must write is exactly what a citation would ground.
An agent could propose Yes/Partial/No with the sentence it read and the
document it came from, and the person confirms or overrides.

Guardrails it needs: it proposes, never records — the click stays the
answer (G-39a). Every proposal carries a verbatim quote and its source; a
proposal it cannot ground is surfaced as a question, not an answer. It may
never write the note on the person's behalf, because the note is the
person's account of their own control.

Nothing here forecloses it: the answer and its note are stored together as
one value, so a drafted proposal has somewhere to put its evidence without
a schema change, and `source` already distinguishes how an answer arrived.
