# UAT — S2.5

**Done when:** Switching persona changes what the platform permits, not only what it shows; every new answer records who gave it.

Every requirement this slice owns gets a row below. Fill each one with what
was actually done and actually observed — "works" is not evidence. A row

### FR-25 — Three roles (requester · Risk Assessor · administrator) with authority enforced server-side, and a persona switcher that demonstrates them

- **What was done:** Switched between all three seeded people in the app bar and attempted the administration surface as each, including by URL rather than by following a link.
- **What was observed:** As a requester the "Agents" link is absent AND visiting `/admin/agents` directly returns "This page is for administrators", naming the current person and role — the refusal is a server decision, not a hidden link. As an administrator the page renders. The Risk Assessor is a distinct third role with neither the admin surface nor the requester's default. The switcher states plainly that it is a pilot device and not a sign-in.
- **Result:** pass
- **Evidence:** `e2e/personas.spec.ts` walks all three personas and asserts the direct-URL refusal; `curl` with a requester cookie returns the refusal page while an admin cookie returns the transparency page. `test/unit/people.test.ts` pins the authority matrix — a requester may never attest (§5.5).

### NFR-19 — Every answer records who gave it; authority is decided by role in one pure module, never in a screen

- **What was done:** Answered a gate as a requester and inspected the stored row; attempted to store an answer attributed to a person who does not exist; confirmed the authority rules live in one module with no framework imports.
- **What was observed:** A person's answer carries `answered_by`. An unknown author is rejected by the foreign key. Intake-derived pre-fills carry a null author deliberately — attribution belongs to whoever confirms them on the gate screen, not to whoever happened to be browsing. `src/lib/people.ts` imports nothing.
- **Result:** pass
- **Evidence:** `test/integration/invariants.test.ts` proves attribution, the unknown-author rejection, and the deliberate null case against real SQL; `test/unit/architecture.test.ts` keeps the role module free of framework, driver and environment imports.

## Findings

- **Note:** answers written before this slice keep a null author. Answers are insert-only, so they cannot be attributed after the fact, and fabricating an author would be worse than admitting the gap. They read as "recorded before attribution existed". All such rows are pilot data.
- **Note (deliberate):** the persona switcher is not authentication and anyone can switch to an administrator. The role check it feeds is genuine and server-side; only the identification is pilot-grade. Both the switcher and the transparency page say so.
