### FR-50 — The assessment carries an **inherent** and a **residual** risk rating — Low, Medium, High or Critical — each a band with every reason that holds, computed by the one engine from rules held as versioned instrument data (`rating.json`); the residual moves by whole bands for open findings, lapsed acceptances, overdue fixes and full attestation; nothing is stored, and the rating is frozen only into the package

- **What was done:** `src/data/instrument/rating.json` — a versioned edition (`risk-rating@2026-09-03.2` — the second, after the verifier found the first edition's Medium reason misdescribed the record; both seeded and activated like the others, the first immutable) holding the inherent rules, the control weights, the residual steps and the appetite lines, every rule a condition the S14 engine evaluates and the S14 lint accepts at import. `src/lib/rating.ts` derives the namespace the rules read (`worst.severity`, `areas.atHigh`, `area.<key>.severity`, `findings.*`, `controls.*`) and computes `inherentRating` (worst matching rule wins, every matching reason kept), `residualRating` (from the inherent band by whole steps, each step naming its reason, clamped to the scale), `areaRatings` and `appetiteBreaches`; the engine owns the order of the bands (`worstBand` in `conditions.ts`) so the architecture rule against a second rank map still holds. `src/lib/rating-of.ts` composes one assessment's rating from what the screens already load — answers, intake, findings with the newest settlement, attestations with the attested value. `RatingChip` renders a band as a word with its reasons behind a native `<details>`; placed on the severity ledger ("Inherent so far"), the controls screen, the submitted view, the review header, the report ("How this rates", with the per-area bands), each row of the reviewer's queue, and the package screen; the package payload freezes `rating` (inherent, residual, appetite breaches, edition).
- **What was observed:** Walked on :3100 as the requester and as a Security assessor. *Sable claims triage* (three policy breaches, nothing signed): Inherent **High** — "at least one severity answer is Medium or higher", "…is High" (re-read on :3100 after edition .2 was activated; the first edition said "is Medium" of a record with no Medium answer — verifier B1); Residual **Critical** — "inherent rating High", "an answer contradicts a policy clause and nothing has settled it", "a high-weight control is not in place…" — with "Exceeds appetite — the line is High; this is Critical. The controls screen shows the inherent only (no findings exist before submission); the ledger's "Inherent so far" reads Medium on the test assessment. No number appears anywhere; every band is a word on a tint with its reasons one click away.
- **Result:** pass

### FR-51 — **Appetite** is instrument data — a band a scope (the assessment, or one risk area) may not exceed, with a reason and where it escalates — and every place a rating exceeds it says so, as a routing signal and never a gate

- **What was done:** Appetite is data in the same edition: a scope (`assessment`, or `area:<key>`), the band it may not exceed, a reason, and where a breach escalates (`role:admin` or `domain:<key>`). `appetiteBreaches` compares the residual for the assessment and each area's worst-severity band for an area line. The chip says "Exceeds appetite" beside the band and, opened, the line, the band and the person it goes to — in words, never the key. - **Result:** pass
- **Evidence:** `test/unit/rating.test.ts` — "appetite" (3); `test/unit/rating-of.test.ts` — "flags appetite on the assessment and on an area"; the walk above.



## Findings

- **The queue rates every row with four reads per assessment** (answers in one query for all rows; then findings, settlements and signatures per row). A reviewer's list is short and the page stays under a second locally, but this is the shape S22's register must not inherit — it moves the rating's inputs into one query there. Severity: low; recorded as a deferral.
- **The first edition's rules are the owner's to ratify** (G-78). They are the reading: which severities make Critical, which controls carry high weight, where the appetite lines sit. Built as data so ratifying them is an edit and a new version, not code.
- **The residual before submission equals the inherent** (no findings exist yet), so the controls screen shows the inherent only — a screen that showed two identical bands would invite the question "why two?".
- **An appetite line could escalate to nobody** — found by the verifier's probe before the API cut it off. `validate()` checked the band, the scope and the reason of every appetite line but not `escalateTo`, so an edition saying `"nowhere"` loaded, and the chip would have rendered its fallback "a reviewer" — a breach routed to no one. Fixed: an edition is refused unless the target is an administrator or a risk area that exists (`rating.ts`, three cases in `rating.test.ts`).
- **A severity word outside the scale reached an area chip** — the same probe. `severitiesOf` took any string as a band, so a stored `"Extreme"` rated its area "Extreme" while the assessment stayed Low. The action refuses such a value at the door (`severity.ts`) and no CHECK backs it, so this was defence in depth: `severitiesOf` now drops anything not Low, Medium or High (`rating-of.ts`, tested).
- **Not defects, for the record:** the probe also flagged a digit in the rating — it was the edition string (now `risk-rating@2026-09-03.2`), which no chip prints; every rendered chip on the review, submitted, report and queue screens was scanned and carried none. Overdue is strict (`due < now`) in the queue SQL, the disposition line and `findingStanding` alike; settlements arrive newest-first from `dispositionsFor`; all-N-A, mixed N-A and a corrected garbage answer hold at the inherent band without moving or crashing.

- **The verifier's first pass was a FAIL (2026-09-03), and this is what it found and what changed.** *B1 — a reason that was not true of the record:* the Medium rule's reason said "at least one severity answer is Medium" for a condition meaning Medium or above, so an assessment whose only severity answer was High displayed a Medium answer that did not exist, on six surfaces. Fixed as a new edition (`risk-rating@2026-09-03.2`; the first is activated and immutable); a test pins the sentence. *B2 — the rating read severity answers the product had removed:* `severitiesOf` iterated every severity question, so unticking a part left its High answer inside the inherent band and the area band while the severity screen no longer asked it and the ledger read "0 answered" — the product contradicting itself in one viewport, with no screen left to change the answer on. Fixed: the rating reads only the questions the assessment is currently asking, derived exactly as the ledger and the controls derive them; a unit test and the browser test cover the untick. *S1/S2:* the validator now refuses a residual step that is not a whole number of bands within the scale, and an inherent rule with no band. *S3/N2:* a breach carries its scope in words and where it escalates into the package, and the package screen prints the word, not the key. *S4:* the band inks are tokens in `base.css` and every ink clears 4.5:1 on its tint (Low 5.3, High 5.2, Critical 5.3). *S5:* `e2e/rating.spec.ts`. *S6:* "inherent" is taught in a line under the chip on the controls screen and the severity ledger. *S7:* §19 has a "Risk rating" block. Amended under G-78.
- **Deferred, recorded:** *N1* — an overdue fix (+1) and a full attestation (−1) can both fire and cancel, listing two reasons that read as contradictory; deterministic by rule order, and at the ends of the scale the order decides. The edition's rules are the owner's to ratify (G-78), and that ratification is where this pair should be resolved — either by an ordering the note states, or by a rule that says which wins. *N3* — `areas.atMedium` is derived and linted and no shipped rule reads it; it is there for an author, not dead. *N5* (a dev-mode SQL fragment in a not-found payload, `project-access.ts`) and *N7* (file budgets: `section-form.tsx`, `assistant.tsx`, `assistant.css`, and `assess.css` at 1196 on this branch) predate or sit outside S15 and are owed elsewhere.
