## Agentic opportunity

The rubric is the seam the drafting layer plugs into. Every criterion it
reports is a fact about the record — was the answer explained, has it
changed, were the detail questions filled in — and one of them,
`draftedWithEvidence`, is deliberately null today with the words "Nothing
drafts answers yet, so there is no evidence trail to weigh" on screen. When
§7's drafting arrives, that criterion stops being null and the same strip
carries it, with the same first line: mechanical checks, not model
confidence.

The guardrail this slice establishes and the agentic layer must inherit:
**the band orders the queue and does nothing else.** It cannot gate, skip or
pre-approve an attestation, and no drafted answer may arrive attested. A
model that scores its own output and a reviewer who reads that score as
permission is precisely the failure this rubric was rebuilt to avoid
(G-61).
